# Example study protocol (synthetic teaching material)

> This is a deliberately generic sample protocol for testing file ingestion. It is not a preregistration and contains no empirical findings.

## Topic
Evidence traceability in AI-assisted scientific question answering.

## Research question
How do retrieval and citation-attribution methods affect the inspectability of answers to scientific questions?

## Proposed design
A comparative evaluation of retrieval and attribution configurations using a manually curated question set and a pre-registered annotation rubric.

## Planned outcomes
- Evidence support judged by two independent annotators.
- Citation precision and citation completeness.
- Unsupported-claim rate.

## Not yet specified
- Sampling frame and corpus size.
- Annotator qualification and adjudication process.
- Statistical analysis plan.
- Ethics review determination and data governance.

Do not represent this sample as a completed study.
