# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and intellectually honest community.

Expected behavior includes constructive feedback, respect for different backgrounds and expertise, clear disagreement without personal attacks, attribution of others' work, and transparency about uncertainty and evidence.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, intimidation, doxxing, plagiarism, knowingly fabricated research claims, and sharing private information without consent.

Project maintainers may remove content or participation that violates these standards. For conduct concerns, contact the repository maintainer privately through the contact method published on the repository hosting account.
