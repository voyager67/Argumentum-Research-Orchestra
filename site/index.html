Why ARGUMENTUM

More than an AI writing interface.

The system is designed around research process, evidence provenance and critical review—not just generating fluent text.

01

Traceable evidence

Screen retrieved sources, preserve identifiers and connect draft claims to a structured evidence ledger.

02

Methodological challenge

Review design fit, sampling, measurement, validity threats, missing methods and inference boundaries.

03

Reproducible outputs

Keep research charters, source registers, synthesis maps, manuscripts, bibliography files and run manifests.

04

Composable orchestration

Choose the native workflow, LangGraph, CrewAI Flow or Microsoft Agent Framework harness.

05

Researcher-owned infrastructure

Run locally or self-host with PostgreSQL, Redis/RQ and compatible model endpoints.

06

Human accountability

AI outputs remain drafts and review aids. Researchers verify sources, claims, methods and submission requirements.

Research resources

Work with scholarly catalogues.

Connectors include OpenAlex, Crossref, Semantic Scholar, Europe PMC and arXiv. Optional GROBID support extracts scholarly document structure from researcher-supplied PDFs.

Model flexibility

Use your model endpoint.

ARGUMENTUM supports OpenAI-compatible chat-completions endpoints, including local serving options such as Ollama and vLLM. Model-specific JSON compatibility should be checked before a run.
