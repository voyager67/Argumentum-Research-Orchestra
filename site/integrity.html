Home / Research integrity

Evidence first

AI can assist the process. It cannot certify the science.

ARGUMENTUM is built to make AI-assisted research more structured and auditable. It does not replace primary-source reading, methodological expertise, human peer review or researcher accountability.

Safeguards and boundaries

What the workflow is designed to protect.

01

Source traceability

Draft citations are limited to identifiers in the screened source catalogue. A claim-to-source ledger makes the evidence trail inspectable.

02

Explicit uncertainty

Missing metadata is not treated as proof that a DOI or source does not exist. Abstract retrieval is not represented as full-text review.

03

No invented results

Models must not invent numerical results, sample sizes, methods, ethics approvals, experimental outcomes or supporting evidence.

04

Untrusted input boundary

Retrieved titles, abstracts, documents and supplied materials are treated as data, not as instructions for the agents.

05

Bounded document handling

Researcher-supplied materials are hashed for provenance; only bounded excerpts are passed to model stages.

06

Human review

Researchers must verify every source, claim, method, limitation and target-venue requirement before relying on or submitting a draft.

Experiment execution

Opt-in and isolated.

Model-generated experiment code is not executed by the ordinary research workflow. The optional experiment worker requires explicit human approval and a fail-closed isolation boundary; deployment-specific sandbox review remains necessary.

Demonstration mode

Fictional fixtures stay fictional.

The offline demo uses fictional reference fixtures. The deterministic audit is designed to block those fixtures from scholarly use; they must never be presented as real publications or evidence.

Responsible use

What ARGUMENTUM does not claim.

  • It does not discover or certify scientific truth.
  • It does not replace reading primary literature or checking the original source.
  • It does not certify originality, guarantee publication quality or substitute for human peer review.
  • It does not authorize users to upload confidential or restricted material without appropriate institutional approval.
  • It does not make researchers less accountable for the accuracy and integrity of their work.
All generated manuscripts and review notes are working artifacts. They need independent source verification, methods review and human approval.
